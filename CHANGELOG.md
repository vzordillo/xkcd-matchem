# Changelog

All notable changes to `xkcd-matchem` are documented in this file.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added
- **Core package, licensing & CI**: Standalone Python 3.10+ package (`0.1.0`; Apache-2.0 code, CC BY 4.0 documentation and tutorials, bundled `xkcd Script` CC BY-NC 3.0 default font + SIL OFL 1.1 alternates `Patrick Hand` and `Comic Neue`, and 17 bundled sample files), strict `mypy` type checking across all source modules, GitHub Actions CI (`ruff`, `pylint`, `mypy`, `pytest --cov-fail-under=90`), strict Jupyter Book `-W` builds, and PyPI OIDC Trusted Publishing release workflow.
- **3D First Brillouin Zones**: Zero-dependency Wigner–Seitz first Brillouin zone solver (`core.brillouin.compute_brillouin_zone`, `BrillouinZone`, `BrillouinFacet`) with F/I/C primitive reduction, LLL reciprocal reduction, and `HalfspaceIntersection` facet geometry, plus `plot_brillouin_zone()` with high-symmetry k-paths.
- **Catalysis Volcano & Defect Formation Diagrams**: Heterogeneous catalysis Sabatier volcano plotter (`plot_volcano`) with automatic linear leg fitting, apex peak shading, and activity/overpotential inversion, plus semiconductor defect formation energy diagrams (`plot_defect_formation`) computing exact charge-state lower convex envelopes, `(q1/q2)` thermodynamic transition levels, and VBM/CBM band-edge shading.
- **LOBSTER Chemical Bonding (`-pCOHP` / `-ICOHP`)**: Zero-dependency `COHPCAR.lobster` / `COOPCAR.lobster` parser (`read_cohpcar`, `CohpData`) supporting spin-polarized and spin-unpolarized interactions, bundled `si_cohp.lobster` sample, bonding/antibonding lobe plotter `plot_cohp()`, and `xkcd-matchem cohp` CLI subcommand.
- **Crystal-Field Splitting, Electrochemistry, Batteries & Analytical NMR/XPS**: Barycenter-conserving $d$-orbital splitting diagrams (`plot_crystal_field`, `crystal_field_levels`, `fill_crystal_field_orbitals`) across octahedral, tetrahedral, square-planar, trigonal-bipyramidal, and custom levels with high/low-spin Hund filling; IUPAC and US-convention cyclic voltammetry (`plot_cyclic_voltammetry`), Tafel overpotential fits (`plot_tafel`), galvanostatic charge/discharge curves (`plot_battery_profile`), and smoothed differential capacity $dQ/dV$ analysis (`plot_dqdv`); first-order $^1\mathrm{H}$ NMR multiplet splitting trees and step integrals (`plot_nmr`, `expand_nmr_multiplet`), plus XPS core-level deconvolution with iterative Shirley background subtraction (`plot_xps`, `compute_shirley_background`).
- **Turnkey SciComm Layout Recipes**: Ready-to-fill millimeter graphical abstracts (`create_graphical_abstract`, `GraphicalAbstractLayout`), 16:9 presentation slides with optional stick-scientist presenter (`create_talk_slide`, `TalkSlideLayout`), multi-card conference posters (`create_conference_poster`, `ConferencePosterLayout`), and $N$-panel mechanism comic strips (`create_mechanism_comic`, `MechanismComicLayout`).
- **Interactive 3D Studio 2.0 & Quick Scene Switcher**: Curated 9-scene Playground switcher (`build_playground_scenes`, opening by default on cross-hatched `water_homo.cube` with category badges and descriptions), 1-click 8-mode pen-and-ink orbital shading pill bar, trajectory multi-frame playback (`frames` slider, Play/Pause, FPS selector, energy readout), 3D Gaussian CUBE orbital isosurface mesh overlay (`isosurfaces` toggle with Lambert shading), per-atom force/spin vector arrows (`vectors` toggle), offline browser/Jupyter HTML export (`view_structure`, `export_interactive_html`), SVG/PNG export, and matching Python snippet generator.
- **Open-Source Governance**: Added contributor guide (`CONTRIBUTING.md`), security policy (`SECURITY.md`), structured GitHub issue and pull request templates (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`, `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`), and live CI/Docs/Binder badges.
- **3D Orbital & Density Isosurfaces**: Zero-dependency pure-NumPy Kuhn 6-tetrahedra Marching Tetrahedra extractor (`core.isosurface.extract_isosurface`, `IsosurfaceMesh`) and depth-split `plot_isosurface()` with xkcd comic pen-and-ink shading modes (`shade="solid" | "hatch" | "crosshatch" | "contour" | "cross_contour" | "stipple" | "scumble" | "outline"`) plus matching Interactive 3D Studio `iso_shade=` controls.
- **Phonon Band Structures**: Zero-dependency Phonopy `band.yaml` reader (`read_phonopy_bands`, bundled `si_phonon_bands.yaml`), `PhononBandStructure` container (`THz` / `cm-1` / `meV`), and `plot_phonon_bands()` with vermilion imaginary-mode highlighting.
- **Equilibrium 3D Wulff Crystal Shapes**: Symmetry-aware `HalfspaceIntersection` solver (`core.wulff.compute_wulff_shape`, `WulffShape`, `WulffFacet`) and `plot_wulff_shape()`.
- **Multi-Panel Narrative Comic Strips & Schemes**: `ComicPanel`, `ComicStrip`, `draw_speech_bubble()`, `draw_stick_scientist()`, `plot_comic_strip()`, 2D skeletal stereobonds (`wedge`, `dash`, `wavy` via SDF/RDKit), and `plot_reaction_scheme()`.
- **3D Force/Spin Vectors, Defects & Fatbands**: `vectors=` (`"forces"`, `"velocities"`, `"magmom"`, or explicit arrays) in `render_structure_3d`, `plot_crystal`, and `plot_molecule`; `draw_defect_site()`; VASP `PROCAR` parser (`read_procar`) + `plot_bands(fatband=...)`; and `plot_reaction_profile(..., barrier_mode="elementary")`.
- **Slide & Abstract Presets + Commercial-Safe Mode**: 9 built-in presets (`classic`, `notebook`, `poster`, `minimal`, `dark_chalk`, `blueprint`, `talk_16x9`, `talk_dark`, `graphical_abstract`), `XM_COMMERCIAL_SAFE` env var support, dark-theme contrast safeguards across renderers, custom theme/palette registries (`register_preset`, `register_palette`, `register_element_palette`, `style_existing_axes`), and PEP 562 lazy top-level loading.
- **Zero-Dependency Space-Group Table & CLI Expansion**: Closed 61-group ITA symmetry generator table (`data/spacegroups.py`) with Origin Choice 1/2 handling, multi-block CIF parsing (`read_cif_all`), `cKDTree` periodic neighbor search (`core.neighbors`), exact-mm `Board.save(..., exact_mm=True)`, and CLI subcommands (`samples`, `formats`, `info`, `render`, `view`, `xrd`, `bands`, `dos`, `cohp`, `neb`, `ptable`, `phonon`).
- **Verified physical reference data**: CODATA 2022 constants, IUPAC 2021 / CIAAW standard atomic weights, Blue Obelisk BODR covalent radii, Alvarez (2013) & Mantina et al. (2009) van der Waals radii, and Cromer-Mann (LA-3816) + exact H 1s X-ray scattering form factors.
- **29 zero-dependency native parsers & strict validation**: XYZ/extXYZ, VASP (`POSCAR`, `CONTCAR`, `XDATCAR`, `DOSCAR`, `EIGENVAL`, `PROCAR`, `CHGCAR`, `LOCPOT`), LOBSTER (`COHPCAR.lobster`, `COOPCAR.lobster`), Phonopy (`band.yaml`), CIF, PDB, SDF/MOL (V2000), MOL2, Gaussian `.cube`/`.gjf`/`.log`/`.fchk`, ORCA `.inp`/`.out`, Quantum ESPRESSO `pw.in`/`pw.out`/`bands.dat`/`dos.dat`, LAMMPS data & `.lammpstrj`, GROMACS `.gro`/`.xvg`, CP2K `.ener`, and xTB `.out`, plus optional adapters for ASE, pymatgen, cclib, RDKit, MDAnalysis, and MDTraj.
- **Scientific geometry, bond perception & full plotting gallery**: LLL-reduced minimum-image vectors, valence/distance bond-order perception, unwrapped RMSD, exact periodic RDF, continuous band-gap detection, affine CUBE slicing, 118-element Periodic Table, ternary hulls, space-group sunbursts, histograms, GNN structure graphs, MatChem icons, spectroscopy & powder XRD, MD GIF animations (`animate_trajectory`), binary phase hulls, Birch-Murnaghan EOS fits, Pourbaix diagrams, parity/bar/pipeline charts, vintage parchment scrolls, and 14 interactive Jupyter Book tutorials.
